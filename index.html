Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Do I need an appointment?
While we do accept walk-ins if there's an open chair, we highly recommend booking an appointment to guarantee your spot and avoid long wait times.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Cash, QRIS, Debit/Credit Cards, and popular E-wallets (GoPay, OVO, Dana).
How long does a haircut usually take?
A standard classic haircut takes about 30-45 minutes. More complex styles like skin fades or adding a beard trim may take up to an hour to ensure perfection.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes, we provide haircuts for children aged 5 and up. Our barbers are patient and skilled at making kids comfortable.
What products do you use?
We use a curated selection of premium grooming products, including top-tier pomades, clays, beard oils, and styling powders available for purchase in-shop.
Can I request a specific barber?
Absolutely! When you book online or call us, you can choose your preferred barber based on availability.
What is included in the Premium Package?
The Premium Package is the ultimate relaxation experience. It includes a precision haircut, beard trim or hot towel shave, hair wash, scalp massage, black mask facial, and styling.
Do you offer styling advice?
Yes! Our barbers are happy to consult with you to find the best cut for your face shape and hair type, and they'll show you how to style it at home.
